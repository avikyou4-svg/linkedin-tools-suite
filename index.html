How to Format a LinkedIn About Section

A well-formatted LinkedIn About section improves readability, helps recruiters understand your expertise and increases profile engagement. Clean formatting makes your profile easier to scan and more professional.

Why Formatting Matters

Most recruiters spend only a few seconds reviewing a profile. Using spacing, bullet points and concise paragraphs can improve readability and help communicate your value more effectively.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters can a LinkedIn About section have?

LinkedIn allows up to 2600 characters in the About section.

Can I use bullet points in LinkedIn?

Yes. Bullet points improve readability and help highlight key information.

Why does LinkedIn formatting sometimes break?

Extra spaces and unsupported formatting may be removed when content is pasted. Using a formatter helps reduce these issues.
